ChoreNook Privacy Policy

Sayis Studios LLC · Last updated August 16, 2026

ChoreNook is a family chores, rewards, and planning app developed by Sayis Studios LLC ("we," "us"). This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

What ChoreNook does

ChoreNook helps families manage chores, rewards, calendars, and meal plans. It works entirely offline and locally on one device by default. Creating an optional account adds cloud sync so a household's data can be shared across multiple devices.

Information we collect

If you use ChoreNook locally (no account): no information leaves your device. All household, chore, reward, and planning data is stored only in the app's local storage on that device. We collect nothing.

If you create a cloud account, we collect:

  • Account info: the email address and password you sign up with. Your password is never stored in plain text — authentication is handled by our backend provider, Supabase.
  • Household data you create: chore names, schedules, points, rewards, goals, routines, calendar events, meal plans, and parent to-dos entered by you or your family members.
  • Family member profiles: names/nicknames and avatar choices for the kids and parents in your household. We do not require or collect a child's real full name, birthdate, or any identifying detail beyond what a parent chooses to enter.
  • Photo chores (optional): if a chore requires photo proof, the photo a child captures is compressed and stored as part of the household's data. It is used only so a parent can review the completed chore.
  • Parent PIN: salted and hashed before storage; never stored in plain text.

How we use information

Solely to operate ChoreNook's core features for your household — syncing chores, rewards, approvals, points, and calendar events across your family's devices when cloud mode is enabled. We do not sell data, run advertising, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Third parties

  • Supabase, our backend provider, hosts account authentication and cloud household data for accounts that opt into cloud sync.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies.

Children's data

ChoreNook is designed for family use, where a parent creates and manages a household and any child profiles within it. Parents control what information is entered for a child and can delete a child's profile and its data at any time. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children outside of a parent-managed household, and we do not use child data for advertising.

App permissions

  • Camera: used only when a parent enables photo-required chores, so a child can capture proof of a completed chore.
  • Notifications: used only to remind you about calendar events created in the app, scheduled locally on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Local-only households: deleting the app removes all data immediately, since nothing is stored elsewhere.

Cloud accounts: you can delete everything yourself, at any time, from inside the app: open the parent area, go to Settings, and use Delete this family. This immediately and permanently removes the household and everything in it — every kid profile, chore, photo, point, reward and calendar entry — from our servers, on every signed-in device. It cannot be undone, and we keep no backup copy. Only the parent who created the family can do this.

Deleting the family does not by itself close the sign-in account (your email address). To have that removed as well, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your choices

You can use ChoreNook fully offline without ever creating an account. Family members and household data you create can be edited or removed directly within the app at any time.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes, and update the "Last updated" date above.
